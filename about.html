SCOPE

What we do — and what we don’t

Saying clearly what we don’t do is part of doing the rest well.

We do

  • Interior painting — walls, ceilings, trim, doors
  • Exterior painting — stucco, siding, trim
  • Drywall repair and ceiling repair, including water-damage patches
  • Drywall finishing — Level 4 and Level 5 smooth, paint-ready surfaces
  • Texture matching (knockdown, orange peel, skip trowel) and popcorn ceiling removal
  • Cabinet painting for kitchens and bathrooms
  • Drywall and paint repair after storm damage

We don’t

  • Act as a general contractor
  • Structural work, framing or roofing
  • Plumbing or electrical
  • Whole-home remodels that require building permits
  • Quote repairs by phone without seeing the surface — the visit is how we get the scope right

When a project needs those trades, we work as the drywall and finish partner alongside your contractor.

PROCESS

How a project runs

The same five steps for a ceiling patch or a whole interior.

  1. You reach outCall, text, or send the form. We reply during business hours with a clear next step.
  2. We visit and measureEstimates are free. We look at the actual surfaces, measure, and ask only what we cannot see for ourselves.
  3. You get a written scopeWhat will be done, with what products, for what price. You approve it online — no printing, no chasing signatures.
  4. We protect, prepare, and finishFloors and furniture are covered. Surfaces are repaired and textured where needed, then primed and painted. One team, start to finish.
  5. Walkthrough and coverageA final walkthrough with you, a 30-day complimentary touch-up window, and your colors, sheens and batch numbers documented for the future.
AFTER THE WORK

Presiado Complete Coverage

Every project is backed by a four-component coverage stack.

3-Year Limited Workmanship Warranty

Subject to the written terms of your project agreement. Covers peeling, blistering, flaking, or coating separation due to defective workmanship on painted surfaces, per the contracted scope of work.

Manufacturer Paint Warranty (up to 25 years)

Premium coatings from Sherwin-Williams Emerald/Duration and Benjamin Moore Aura include manufacturer warranties of 15–25 years against product defect. Documentation is provided with the completion package.

30-Day Complimentary Touch-Up

Within 30 days of completion, we return at no cost to address minor adjustments identified during normal use.

Lifetime Color Documentation

Exact colors, sheens, and product batch numbers documented for every project, so future touch-ups or matching work always have an accurate reference.

QUESTIONS

Straight answers

What does Presiado Home Improvement do?

Professional painting and drywall for homes in Sarasota, Florida: interior and exterior painting, drywall repair, ceiling repair, Level 4 and Level 5 drywall finishing, texture matching, popcorn ceiling removal, cabinet painting, and drywall and paint repair after storm damage. Drywall and painting are handled as one system by one team.

What does Presiado Home Improvement not do?

We are not a general contractor. We do not do structural work, roofing, plumbing, electrical, or whole-home remodels that require permits. When a project needs those trades, we work as the drywall and finish partner alongside your contractor.

Where do you work?

Sarasota and the surrounding communities: Siesta Key, Longboat Key, Lakewood Ranch, Palmer Ranch, Bird Key, St. Armands, Osprey, Nokomis, Venice and Bradenton. The office is at 4704 Swift Rd, Sarasota, FL 34231.

Are you insured?

Yes. Presiado Home Improvement LLC is an insured Florida LLC. Proof of insurance is available on request before work begins.

What coverage comes with a project?

Presiado Complete Coverage: 3-Yr Workmanship Warranty · Manufacturer Paint Warranty (up to 25 yrs) · 30-Day Touch-Up · Lifetime Color Documentation. The workmanship warranty is subject to the written project agreement.

How does the process work?

You call, text or send the form. We visit to measure and look at the surfaces; estimates are free. You receive a written scope with a price and approve it online. We protect, repair, texture, prime and paint. Then a final walkthrough, a 30-day touch-up window, and your color documentation.

Do you take small projects?

Yes. Most of our projects are between a single repair and a few rooms. The same process applies whether it is one ceiling patch or a whole interior.

Do you work with contractors and property managers?

Yes. We are a recurring drywall and painting partner for kitchen and bath remodelers and building contractors in Sarasota, working under a written work order on their projects.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us about it and we’ll follow up promptly during business hours with a clear next step.
