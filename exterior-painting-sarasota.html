Exterior Painting — Frequently Asked Questions

Exterior painting in Sarasota typically ranges from $1.50 to $4.50 per square foot of paintable surface. A standard 2,000 sq ft home runs $3,500–$7,000. Price depends on surface condition, number of stories, stucco vs siding, prep work required, and paint quality. We provide a detailed itemized estimate after your free on-site consultation — no hidden fees.

We exclusively use Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore exterior-grade products including Emerald Exterior, Duration Exterior, and Aura Exterior — all UV-resistant and mildew-proof, specifically chosen for Florida’s climate. For stucco we use elastomeric coatings that bridge hairline cracks and resist moisture penetration.

Yes — stucco is the dominant exterior surface in Sarasota and we specialize in it. We prepare, prime, and paint stucco with Florida-rated elastomeric coatings. We also repair minor stucco cracks and damage before painting so the final finish is seamless and durable. If your home was affected by a recent storm and the exterior surface shows water damage, see our Post-Storm Drywall & Paint Repair page for interior finish recovery scope.

Yes — always. Professional power washing is included in every exterior project. We never paint over a dirty surface. Power washing removes mold, mildew, chalk, and contaminants that would cause premature paint failure.
