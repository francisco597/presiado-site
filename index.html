Presiado Complete Coverage

Every Presiado project is backed by a four-component coverage stack.

✓

3-Year Limited Workmanship Warranty

Subject to the written terms of your project agreement. Covers peeling, blistering, flaking, or coating separation due to defective workmanship on interior and exterior painted surfaces, per the contracted scope of work.

✓

Manufacturer Paint Warranty (up to 25 years)

Premium coatings from Sherwin-Williams Emerald/Duration and Benjamin Moore Aura include manufacturer warranties of 15–25 years against product defect. Product documentation provided with project completion package.

✓

30-Day Complimentary Touch-Up

Within 30 days of project completion, Presiado returns at no cost to address minor adjustments identified during normal use.

✓

Lifetime Color Documentation

Exact colors, sheens, and product batch numbers documented for every project — so future touch-ups, repairs, or matching work always have an accurate reference, regardless of how many years have passed since original work.
