Consumer Protection

Why Working with a Licensed Florida LLC + Insured Matters

In Florida, the legal structure of the company you hire matters. A registered Florida LLC carrying general liability insurance and workers’ compensation gives you four specific protections by law — protections that an unregistered handyman or out-of-state outfit simply cannot provide.

State Registered LLC

A registered Florida LLC is verifiable on the Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz) database. Company standing, registered agent, and good-standing status are public record — you can verify before you sign.

Insurance Transfers Liability

A registered LLC carrying general liability insurance and workers’ compensation absorbs on-site risk. If something goes wrong — property damage, a worker injury — the financial exposure does not become yours.

Local Permits & Documentation

When the scope requires a permit, we pull it through the proper local channel and keep documentation on file. Clean paper trail for resale, insurance claims, or future work — no surprises down the road.

Written Contracts & Coverage on File

Every project is written: signed proposal, defined scope, defined price. Certificate of insurance and workers’ comp documentation provided on request. Your protection lives in the paperwork, not in a handshake.

Presiado Home Improvement LLC is a fully registered Florida LLC + Insured. Verify our standing — Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz), our certificate of general liability insurance, or our workers’ compensation policy. Documentation provided on request, before any project begins.
