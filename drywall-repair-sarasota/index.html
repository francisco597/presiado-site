FINISH LEVELS

Level 4 vs Level 5 — Which Do You Need?

Most homeowners don’t know the difference until they see it under the wrong light. Here’s what each level delivers.

LEVEL 4

Level 4 Finish

All joints, angles, fasteners, and beads coated and sanded to a clean, smooth surface. The standard for most residential interiors painted with flat or matte finishes.

Best for: Flat and matte paint • Textured finishes • Standard bedrooms & living areas

LEVEL 5

Level 5 Finish

A full skim coat of joint compound applied over the entire surface — the highest quality finish available. Eliminates all tool marks, surface variations, and joint shadows visible under raking or direct light.

Best for: Gloss & semi-gloss paint • High-end interiors • Luxury homes • Critical lighting environments

Not sure which level your project needs? We assess your walls and paint sheen during the free consultation and recommend the correct finish for your specific conditions.

Premium Finish

Level 5 — The Highest Drywall Finish Standard

Drywall is rated on a six-tier scale from Level 0 to Level 5. The construction industry recognizes Level 5 as the highest. It is required when high-gloss paint, raking light, or critical lighting will reveal every imperfection on the surface.

LEVEL 4

Level 4 — Standard Contractor Finish

All joints, fasteners, and beads are taped, coated, and sanded smooth. Suitable for matte and flat paint under normal ambient lighting. The standard finish for most residential interiors and where most drywall scopes end.

LEVEL 5

Level 5 — Skim-Coat Finish

A full skim coat of joint compound applied across the entire wall surface, then sanded to a single uniform plane. Eliminates joint shadows and tool marks, producing a continuous surface that reads as one finished plane under any lighting condition.

WHEN IT MATTERS

When Level 5 Matters

Luxury homes with custom lighting, walls receiving semi-gloss or high-gloss paint, foyers and great rooms with raking light, and museum-quality interiors where the architect specified one continuous surface. In Sarasota’s premium homes, Level 5 is a baseline, not an upgrade.

Not sure which level your project requires? We assess paint sheen, lighting conditions, and surface use during the consultation, then recommend the correct finish for your specific scope.
