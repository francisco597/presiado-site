OUR PROCESS

A Precise, 4-Step Cabinet Process

Every step is deliberate. Every detail is controlled. This is how we achieve a finish your guests will think is new cabinetry.

1

Consultation & Quote

Free on-site visit. We count doors, assess surfaces, and deliver a detailed proposal in 24 hours. No pressure. No hidden costs.

2

Prep & Removal

All doors and drawers are tagged and removed. Surfaces are degreased, sanded, filled, and primed. This is where the finish is won or lost.

3

Spray & Application

Doors are sprayed off-site for factory smoothness. Cabinet boxes are finished on-site with self-leveling enamel and precise brush technique.

4

Reinstall & Walkthrough

All doors and hardware are reinstalled. We walk through the project with you and address every detail until every surface meets our White Glove standard.
