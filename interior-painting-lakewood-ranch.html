The Builder-Grade Upgrade

Most Lakewood Ranch homes are delivered with flat builder paint. Here’s why that matters — and what we do about it.

Builder Paint vs. Premium Paint

Builders use flat interior paint to pass inspection and minimize construction costs. It scratches easily, doesn’t wipe clean, and shows marks within months of move-in. It’s not designed to last.

We repaint over builder-grade surfaces with Sherwin-Williams Duration Home or Benjamin Moore Aura — delivering a washable, durable, and dramatically better finish for Lakewood Ranch homeowners.

  • Washable & scrubbable finish
  • Dramatically richer, more uniform color
  • Level 5 skim coating available for smooth walls
  • Color consultation included
  • HOA-compliant palette navigation
